Check your heart rate with a 30-second selfie video.

Every heartbeat changes the colour of your skin a tiny bit. You can't see it, but a camera can. Fair Vitals picks that up from a short video of your face, and if it isn't confident in the result, it tells you to try again instead of showing a number.

It admits when it's unsure

Each 10 seconds of video gets a trust score. If none of them pass, you'll be asked to record again.

Made with skin tone in mind

Camera-based heart rate tends to work worse on darker skin. Our trust check never looks at skin tone, and we checked results for each skin-tone group. We haven't proved it's fair yet; that needs a bigger test.

Tested on new video

On video it had never trained on, the readings it showed were 2.7 BPM off on average.
