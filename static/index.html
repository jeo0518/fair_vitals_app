Heart rate from a selfie

Your pulse, read from a short video of your face.

Each heartbeat changes the colour of your skin very slightly. It is far too small to see, but a camera can pick it up. Fair Vitals measures that signal in a 20–30 second selfie video, then checks whether it should trust its own answer before showing you a number.

Knows when not to trust itself

Every 10-second reading is scored. You only see a heart rate when it is likely within 5 BPM; otherwise the app asks you to retake.

Built with skin tone in mind

Skin tone is never used to decide whether a reading is trusted, and results were checked per skin-tone group. Proving fairness still needs a larger, balanced test.

Tested on unseen video

On a dataset it never trained on, readings the app accepts averaged 2.7 BPM error, with 90% within 5 BPM.

Try it

Record a 20–30 second selfie video, then upload it.

  • Face the camera with your whole face in the frame.
  • Stay still and hold the phone steady.
  • Use steady, even light (daylight from a window works well).
